# Equations

Inline equations such as $E = mc^{2}$ and $\alpha_{1} + \beta^{2} \leq \sqrt{x}$ stay in the sentence.

$$
\int_{0}^{1} x \, dx = \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

Raw TeX without dollar delimiters, such as \textbf{bold} or \emph{text}, stays ordinary text.
